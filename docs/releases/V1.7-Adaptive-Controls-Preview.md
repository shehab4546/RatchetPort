# RatchetPort V1.7 — Adaptive Controls Preview

Adaptive texture-cache management, configurable keyboard movement, and game-language choices matched to the American edition.

- **Adaptive memory management:** cleanup follows the graphics driver's budget, spreads work across short sweeps, and avoids repeatedly processing blocked resources. Your chosen internal resolution stays unchanged, including 4K.
- **Custom keyboard movement:** remap every direction of both analog sticks alongside your game buttons.
- **Flexible mouse control:** choose the left stick, right stick, or disable mouse movement. Switch between always active and hold-L2 activation, adjust sensitivity, invert the vertical axis, and choose relative or absolute movement. Left-stick mouse control is the default.
- **Language choices for the USA edition:** English, French, Portuguese (Portugal), Portuguese (Brazil), and Latin American Spanish with Mexican Spanish voice. Game language is independent of launcher language, and changes apply after restarting.
- **Fresh identity:** V1.7 and Adaptive Controls Preview appear throughout the launcher and Windows package metadata.

Supports CUSA01047 USA v1.00. Text and voice follow the game's own pairing; independent voice selection is not enabled. This preview targets memory-cleanup overhead and control flexibility. Actual smoothness and performance vary by device and scene, and tiled image writeback remains unsupported.
