# RatchetPort V1.7 — Adaptive Controls Preview

More control over image quality, camera movement and the language you hear.

- **Five graphics presets:** Low, Medium, High, Very High and Ultra coordinate render resolution, output size, shadows, filtering and motion blur, up to 4K. Fine-tune any option to create a Custom setup.
- **Independent text and voice:** choose English, French, Portuguese (Portugal/Brazil), or Latin American Spanish text with Mexican Spanish voice support. Mixed-language selection is experimental on the verified USA v1.00 executable.
- **Voice archive diagnostics:** quick checks identify missing files and obvious repeated-block corruption. Clear warnings explain why dialogue may be missing while allowing the game to start.
- **Simpler mouse camera:** assign mouse movement directly in the stick-direction fields of the control map. Keyboard diagonals combine both directions; mouse movement keeps its angle with radial dead-zone compensation. Stopping the mouse releases the stick. Separate relative/absolute mode panels are removed.
- **Audio timing diagnostics:** logs now show feeding gaps, starvation and queued audio duration. Excessive queued audio is discarded to prevent a long stale backlog after a stall; normal decoding is unchanged.
- **Resolution diagnostics:** inspect sample counts and capture graphics activity across multiple presentation frames, including repeated interface passes and color-binding details for black-world reports. Scene-size observations remain conditional on recognition of a matching pass.
- **Experimental high-resolution memory reserve:** 1440p and 4K receive additional space for native rendering resources, with allocation failures visible in the log. The extra reserve increases startup memory use by 384 MiB or 768 MiB respectively.
- **Rendering stability:** large deferred command batches now keep their copied data alive across recording-chunk boundaries, preventing a use-after-free when a chunk fills up.
- **Cleaner settings:** graphics options are grouped together, mouse directions share the keyboard control map, and Save and Play keep their space when status messages grow.
- **Adaptive memory management:** texture-cache cleanup follows the graphics driver's budget and spreads work across bounded sweeps while preserving the selected resolution.

Supports CUSA01047 USA v1.00 with your own game files. Save and restart after changing languages or rendering options. Mixed-language gameplay and cinematic playback require testing; sample checks do not certify a complete audio archive. Performance varies by device and scene, and tiled image writeback remains unsupported.

Known issue: explicit internal resolutions can render a black 3D world while HUD/menus remain visible (issue #19). The larger native heap targets a reproduced allocation-failure path; resolution of the reported black world and 1440p/4K gameplay remain unverified.

The RX 570 startup crash (issue #24) remains under investigation. The command-data lifetime correction is covered by a regression test, but has not yet been verified against that crash on the affected hardware.
