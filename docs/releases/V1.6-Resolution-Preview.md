# V1.6 — Resolution Preview

Choose the scene detail that suits your setup. **Resolution Preview** brings
independent internal rendering controls to RatchetPort, from 720p through an
experimental **4K — 3840 × 2160** mode.

## Scene resolution up to 4K

Pick **720p, 900p, 1080p, 1440p or 4K**, or keep **Original** to follow the game's
own settings. Lower resolutions reduce the number of scene pixels rendered;
higher resolutions offer more detail at a greater GPU and memory cost.

Internal rendering and output resolution have separate controls, so scene detail
can be adjusted without changing the window size. Changes take effect after a
game restart.

## Clearer graphics information

The in-game panel now separates the startup resolution, observed scene/depth
candidates and viewport, final presented image and window size. Missing
observations remain clearly marked, making resolution comparisons easier.

## Your visual settings, together

The new resolution selector appears on **Home** and **Graphics**, alongside
motion blur and **1024 / 512 / 256 px** shadow-map caps. Existing **30 FPS / Original**
and **60 FPS (experimental)** options remain available. Each visual control can
return to Original independently, and saved resolution preferences survive
changes made through the in-game menu.
